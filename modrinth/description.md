# Dramatic Deaths

**Every death is a movie scene.**

When you die, the game drops into slow motion, the camera breaks free from your body and a short cinematic plays, made for exactly how you died. Then a dark end screen gives you the title, a random line and your own Respawn and Leave buttons.

## A different scene for every death

- **Killed by a mob or player**: a **killcam**. The camera whips round to whoever killed you, circles them from a low "villain" angle, then cranes up over both of you. A card slides in with their name, health, weapon and distance.
- **Explosion**: you get blasted backwards, tumbling, through smoke and ash.
- **Fall damage**: a vertigo zoom straight down on your body while the camera rises.
- **Drowning, fire, freezing, the void, lightning, anvils, magic, starving** each have their own camera move, color grade, particles and sounds.

## Titles that react to how you died

The big title isn't just text. Something happens to it:

| Death | Title |
|---|---|
| Lava / fire | The letters catch fire and burn away from the bottom |
| Drowning | The letters fall into water and sink |
| Anvil | An anvil drops on the word and flattens it |
| Starving | Bites get taken out of the letters, then a burp |
| Mob with a weapon | Their weapon slices through the word |
| Arrows | Arrows thunk into the letters |
| Explosion | A TNT lands, blinks, and blows the letters apart |
| Fall | The letters crash into the ground and crack |
| Lightning | A bolt splits the middle letter |
| Freezing | Ice blocks grow around each letter |
| Void | The letters slip into a rift |
| Magic | A splash potion smashes on the word and stains it |

## Never the same twice

Over 200 hand-written lines (English and German), with lines for about 30 mobs. Drown twice and you'll get two different lines, and the same one never comes twice in a row.

## Details

- **Skip** the scene at any time with any key or click (after a short moment, so mid-fight clicks don't skip it).
- **Hardcore support**: the button becomes *Spectate World*, and you can always leave the world.
- **Client-side only**: works on any server, including vanilla servers. Nobody else needs it.
- **No dependencies**: only needs Fabric Loader, not Fabric API.
